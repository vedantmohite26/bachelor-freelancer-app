## 2026-06-15 - Leaderboard list virtualization & provider lookup hoisting
**Learning:** In Flutter, nesting `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true, physics: NeverScrollableScrollPhysics())` inside a `SingleChildScrollView` forces Flutter to instantiate, layout, and render every single list item at once, completely disabling list virtualization. Combining `CustomScrollView` with `DecoratedSliver`, `SliverPadding`, and `SliverList.builder` preserves custom list container decorations (like top rounded background corners) while maintaining $O(1)$ lazy element construction. Additionally, fetching `currentUserId` via `Provider.of<AuthService>(context, listen: false)` once outside the item builder delegate eliminates $O(N)$ repeated inherited widget traversals.
**Action:** Always replace `SingleChildScrollView` + `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true)` with `CustomScrollView` + `SliverList.builder` (or `DecoratedSliver`), and hoist context/provider lookups outside list item builder loops.
