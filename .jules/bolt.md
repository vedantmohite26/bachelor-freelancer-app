## 2026-06-15 - Flutter List Virtualization & Provider Lookups in Scroll Delegates
**Learning:** Combining `SingleChildScrollView` with `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true, physics: NeverScrollableScrollPhysics())` forces Flutter to instantiate and measure all list items at once, disabling list virtualization and causing substantial frame drops as the dataset grows. Furthermore, executing `Provider.of<T>(context, listen: false)` inside the `itemBuilder` callback causes repeated provider lookups for every item rendered during scroll cycles.
**Action:** Replace `SingleChildScrollView` + `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true)` hierarchies with `CustomScrollView` wrapping `SliverToBoxAdapter` for fixed content and `SliverList` (or `SliverList.builder`) wrapped in `DecoratedSliver` for lists. Always extract static provider lookups (such as `currentUserId`) outside the item builder delegate.
