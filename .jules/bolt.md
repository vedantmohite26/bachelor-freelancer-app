## 2026-10-24 - HelperReviewsScreen Nested ListView Anti-Pattern
**Learning:** Found a nested list anti-pattern in `HelperReviewsScreen` where a `SingleChildScrollView` wrapped a `Column` containing a `ListView.builder` with `shrinkWrap: true` and `NeverScrollableScrollPhysics()`. This disabled Flutter's default item virtualization, forcing it to layout and build all reviews up-front, severely hurting performance for users with many reviews.
**Action:** Replace `SingleChildScrollView` + `Column` + `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true)` with a single `CustomScrollView` wrapped by a `StreamBuilder`. Convert static segments like summaries/dividers into `SliverToBoxAdapter` slivers, loading/error states into centered `SliverFillRemaining` placeholders, and the review list itself into a virtualized `SliverPadding` wrapping a `SliverList` delegate.
