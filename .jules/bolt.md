## 2026-11-20 - Nested Scroll Virtualization with Stream/Future Builder
**Learning:** In Flutter, nesting scrollable views (like ListView) inside other scrollables (like SingleChildScrollView) requires `shrinkWrap: true` and `NeverScrollableScrollPhysics()`, which completely disables viewport virtualization and causes immediate construction of all list elements. However, converting this to a `CustomScrollView` with slivers while hosting asynchronous builders requires carefully wrapping status placeholders (loading/error/empty) in `SliverFillRemaining(hasScrollBody: false)` to maintain visual centering without viewport breaks.
**Action:** When optimizing lists inside a scrolling layout, leverage `CustomScrollView` + `SliverToBoxAdapter` for non-list headers, and dynamically switch between `SliverFillRemaining` for status placeholders and `SliverPadding` + `SliverList` for virtualized items.
