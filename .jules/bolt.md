## 2026-08-25 - Caching Review Streams & Virtualizing Layout in HelperReviewsScreen
**Learning:** In review/ratings screens, using `SingleChildScrollView` with `ListView.builder(shrinkWrap: true, physics: NeverScrollableScrollPhysics())` causes Flutter to calculate heights and build all review cards upfront, disabling list virtualization and forcing double layout passes. Furthermore, placing stream calls directly inside `build()` without caching in `didChangeDependencies()` causes `StreamBuilder` to unsubscribe and re-subscribe to Firestore listeners whenever parent widgets rebuild or theme/orientation changes occur.
**Action:** Convert review list screens to `StatefulWidget`, cache Firestore streams in `didChangeDependencies()` with an initialization flag, replace nested scroll views with a `CustomScrollView` (`SliverToBoxAdapter` + `SliverList.builder`), and wrap list item widgets in `RepaintBoundary` to isolate repaints during scrolling.
