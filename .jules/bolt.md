## 2026-03-31 - Firestore Stream Caching Across TabBar and TabBarView
**Learning:** When a screen uses a `TabBar` with count badges and a `TabBarView` with list views, invoking stream getter methods directly in `build()` creates multiple active Firestore listeners per frame. Converting the screen to a `StatefulWidget` and storing stream instances in `initState()` prevents `StreamBuilder` from tearing down and recreating subscriptions. In test mocks for shared broadcast streams across TabBar badges and lazy TabBarView pages, `Stream.multi` ensures every subscriber receives initial data events when attaching.
**Action:** Always cache stream properties in state for screens with TabBar/TabBarView or header badges to eliminate duplicate Firestore connections and re-subscription overhead.
